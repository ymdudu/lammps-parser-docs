FramesHandler

A module for Frames (as a series of BaseFrame, i.e. a trajectory) information.

Note

Functionality:

  1. store a specific trajectory in configuration space, which consists of multiple BaseFrames, into one variable.

  2. perform some calculations involving more than one BaseFrames.
